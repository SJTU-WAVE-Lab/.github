# WAVE Lab
## Wireless & Autonomous Vehicle Engineering Lab


Welcome to the GitHub home of **WAVE Lab**. Our research focuses on **marine robotics, reinforcement learning, and wireless communications**, with the goal of building autonomous systems that can learn, communicate, and operate reliably in complex ocean environments.

The ocean presents challenges that cannot be addressed by autonomy or connectivity alone. We explore how intelligent decision-making, robotic systems, and wireless networks can work together to support marine exploration, observation, and cooperative operations. We aim to connect advances in algorithms with practical engineering, from simulation and system development to real-world validation.

欢迎来到 **WAVE Lab（Wireless & Autonomous Vehicle Engineering Lab）** 的 GitHub 主页。我们聚焦**海洋机器人、强化学习与无线通信**，致力于探索复杂海洋环境下自主系统的智能决策、可靠通信与协同作业。

海洋环境中的不确定性、有限的通信条件与复杂的动力学，使机器人自主运行面临独特挑战。我们关注学习、控制与通信之间的联系，努力将算法研究与系统实践结合，让机器人能够更好地感知环境、学习策略、交换信息并完成任务。

## Research Directions | 研究方向

- **Marine Robotics | 海洋机器人**  
  Autonomous navigation, motion planning, control, and cooperative operation for underwater and surface robotic systems.  
  面向水下与水面机器人，研究自主导航、运动规划、控制与多机器人协同。

- **Reinforcement Learning | 强化学习**  
  Learning-based decision-making and control, with an emphasis on robustness, adaptation, and transfer from simulation to real robotic systems.  
  探索基于强化学习的决策与控制方法，关注策略的鲁棒性、环境适应能力以及从仿真到真实机器人的迁移。

- **Wireless Communications | 无线通信**  
  Underwater acoustic and optical communications, networked robotic systems, and communication-aware coordination.  
  研究声光通信、网络化机器人系统，以及考虑通信约束的协同方法。
